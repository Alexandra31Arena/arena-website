What you want to know first

Do I need experience to start?

No. ARENA is built to take you from zero to your first real client meeting with confidence. Level 1 starts with the simplest phone calls.

Which devices does it work on?

Phone and laptop, straight from the browser. Train whenever you have 10 free minutes.

Is ARENA available in the Emirates?

Yes. The English edition is built for agents in Dubai and across the Emirates. Create your account, sign in from your phone or laptop and start with Level 1.

Can I train my whole team?

Yes. Agencies get volume packages at a reduced price and a manager dashboard to follow every agent's progress. Write to us at contact@arenaforagents.com for agency pricing.

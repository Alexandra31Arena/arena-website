What is ARENA

A simulator where mistakes don't cost you clients

Practice real conversations with simulated clients — so that by the time you meet the real one, you have already been there dozens of times.

ARENA is a hands-on sales training for real estate agents, built as a conversation simulator. Instead of learning the job at the expense of your first real clients, you practice in a safe space: you talk to simulated clients who behave like real people — they hesitate, object, change their minds, and bring their own emotions and stories.

The difference from a classic course is simple: here you don't listen to how it's done — you do it. You run the whole conversation, from the first "hello" to the moment of signing, and you get an honest verdict at the end.

Sample conversation in ARENA

The owner

Yes, go on. I'd rather know everything, even if I don't always like what I hear.
South-west facing, new double glazing, rewired electrics, no outstanding service charges — these are real selling points I'll put in front of buyers.

The owner

I'm glad you noticed — I've looked after this place as best I could. And what's not so good?

The levels

From the first call to the signature

Two levels, built like a real path: first you win the meeting over the phone, then you take it all the way to an exclusive listing.

Level 1

The phone call

It all starts on the phone. You face 15 clients, from the most relaxed to the nightmare who hangs up in three seconds. One goal only: get past "I'm not interested" and land the meeting.

15 clients  ·  increasing difficulty  ·  certificate at the end
Level 2

The face-to-face meeting

You're in the client's home. You do the tour, read the signals, negotiate the price and go all the way to the exclusivity signature. 9 characters, each with their own story, reason for selling and objections.

9 characters  ·  tour + price + exclusivity  ·  verdict at the end
Level 2 · The three phases

The tour, the price, the exclusivity

1 · The tour

You read the signals in every room, ask technical questions and uncover the real reason for selling — without commenting on taste or defects out loud.

2 · The price

You anchor the value in real data — comparable transactions, floor level, finishes — and tie the price to the client's life, not just to numbers.

3 · The exclusivity

You defuse the objections, propose the term, explain the termination clause openly — then you ask for the signature and let the silence do its work.
